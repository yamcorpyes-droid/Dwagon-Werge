      🐉 How to play

      • Drag items around green land. Drag empty ground to move the map; pinch, mouse wheel or +/− to zoom.
      • On a tablet, double-tap or press and hold an item to use it (that's "double-click" below). Tapping an item also shows its buttons at the top of the map.
      • Put 3 matching items next to each other (up/down/left/right) to merge them. Merge 5 at once and you get 2! Sometimes a Bonus Bubble appears too: pop it with 🌙 within a minute, or it floats off and leaves a coin.
      • A 🌈 Rainbow Prism counts as any item in a merge.
      • Double-click the 🧺 Seed Basket for Life Sprouts. It holds 10 and regrows one every couple of minutes, even while the game is closed. Merge 3 baskets for a bigger, faster one.
      • Flowers and trees regrow their charges over time too, and dwagons spend ⚡ energy on each harvest and rest in nests to recover. A 🍓 treat perks them right up.
      • 🌙 Moonstones skip any wait: refill a basket or plant, wake a dwagon, pop a bubble or pick a lock. You only ever earn them, from quests, Adventures, 5-merges and Moonstone Geodes.
      • Golden Chests and Dwagon Hoards are locked: a 🔑 Chest Key on the board opens them.
      • Double-click flowers and trees to make ✨ Life Essence. Click an orb to heal the dead land closest to it.
      • Merge 3 eggs to hatch a dwagon. There are 6 breeds (Meadow, Stone, Tide, Ember, Bloom and Star), each with its own special ability and 8 levels. Merging three level-4 dwagons makes a Cradle: tap it to hatch a level 5.
      • Double-click rocks, 🍄 mushrooms or 🌿 grass and a dwagon flies over to harvest them. Idle dwagons also harvest things right next to them by themselves (in Adventures they leave alone anything a bonus goal still needs). In camp, dwagons need Nests to sleep in, or they nap instead of working.
      • 🐲 Dwagon Power is the strength of the dwagons in your camp. It clears mist (Stars do too) and gives you more storage. See it all in the 🐲 Dwagon Book.
      • Gloomlings roam the dead land and make it harder to heal. Heal the tile they stand on to banish them. Heal a 🗿 Gloom Totem to stop it summoning more.
      • The 🗺 World Map has 30 Adventures in 5 worlds. Each hides a broken 🗿 Guardian Statue under its dead land: heal your way to the pieces and merge them to win. That's ⭐ 1 Star; the bonus goals and healing all the land give ⭐ 2 more. Stars clear the mist around your camp.
      • Starting an Adventure uses a 🏆 Chalice (coming back to one you left is free). You win Chalices back by finishing Adventures, from quests, Goal Stars and Wonders, or buy one in the Market with coins. Never with money.
      • Click coins, bricks, bubbles and chests to collect or open them. Spend what you earn in the 🛒 Market. Keep spare camp items in 📦 Storage.
      • ✨ Wonders sit at the top of some merge chains (World Tree, Everbloom Garden, Toadstool Palace, Sunstone Circle, Dwagon Roost, Moon Altar). They add lots of Dwagon Power and make useful things.
      • Some mist has a cloud key: it shows an item, and dropping that item on the mist clears it.
      • You can only hold so many coins. 🏺 Coin Jars (merge them into Coffers and Vaults) hold more.
      • Every finished quest also gives a ⭐ Goal Star: tap it 5 times for 5 prizes. In Adventures, 🌠 Moon Stars fall now and then; collect them, merge them, or leave them to fly home when you finish.
      • Heal every tile of your camp to win.

      No real money, ever. Everything is earned by playing. 💚

      Each Adventure hides a broken 🗿 Guardian Statue under its dead land. Heal your way to the pieces and merge them to win. Starting an Adventure uses a 🏆 Chalice; leaving and coming back later is free.

      📦 Storage

      Select an item in camp and press Store to tuck it away. Take it out any time.

      🐲 Dwagon Book

      Merge 3 eggs to hatch a dwagon, and 3 dwagons for the next level. Three level-4 dwagons make a Cradle: tap it to hatch a level-5. Tap a dwagon you own to find it on the map.
